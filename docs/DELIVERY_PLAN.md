## Proposed release gates

1. Requirements review: approve scope, criteria, zone policy and owners.
2. Integration readiness: validate telemetry and notification contracts and failure handling.
3. User acceptance: demonstrate desktop/mobile workflows, permissions and failure scenarios.
4. Operational readiness: approve load evidence, recovery plan, monitoring and safety procedures.
5. Release decision: record outstanding defects, residual risk and accountable approval.

The prototype satisfies only its documented simulation scope. It does not pass these operational gates.

## Change control example

Proposed CR-01 adds delivery receipts to emergency notifications. Capture the trigger, affected story US-7, provider dependency, implementation estimate, acceptance tests and schedule impact. The product owner prioritises value. Release and safety leads review operational risk. Record the decision and update the backlog and schedule if approved. No actual approval is implied.
